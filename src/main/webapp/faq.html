Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ordering, delivery, and our authentic Italian dining experience.

How do I place an order? expand_more

You can place an order through our website by going to the Menu page, choosing your dishes, adding them to your cart, and proceeding to checkout.

Do I need to log in before checkout? expand_more

Yes. Customers can browse the menu freely, but they need to log in or register before confirming an order so their cart, contact details, and order history can be saved correctly.

Can I change quantities or add-ons before checkout? expand_more

Yes. You can adjust item quantities, remove dishes, or review selected add-ons in your cart before proceeding to checkout and placing the final order.

How do I know my order is confirmed? expand_more

After checkout, the website will display an order confirmation page with the ordered items, total amount, and confirmation message. This lets you know the order has been submitted successfully.

Can I cancel my order? expand_more

Orders can be cancelled within 15 minutes of placement. For immediate assistance, please contact our support line directly. Refunds may take 3-5 business days depending on your bank.

Do you offer delivery? expand_more

Yes, we offer delivery within a 5-mile radius of our location. The delivery fee will be calculated at checkout based on the customer address.

What payment methods are accepted? expand_more

We accept major credit and debit cards. For in-person dining or pickup, we also accept cash.

What are your operating hours? expand_more

We are open Tuesday through Saturday from 12:00 PM to 11:30 PM. On Sundays, we are open from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. We are closed on Mondays.

Our team is here to help ensure your experience is nothing short of authentic.
